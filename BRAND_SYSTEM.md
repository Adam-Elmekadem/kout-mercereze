# Mercereze brand system

## Position

**Idea:** Tailoring in motion. Mercereze treats movement, control, and precision as fashion construction cues. It is an independent fictional fashion label in the Kout Project portfolio, not an official collaboration.

**Audience:** Design-aware shoppers and creative directors who value precise outerwear, editorial presentation, and technical detail.

**Promise:** Clothing with a composed silhouette and useful movement.

**Voice:** Composed, exact, and short. Speak about fabric, fit, and form. Avoid automotive slogans, invented performance claims, and vague luxury superlatives.

## Identity

The split-M monogram uses two rising strokes with a deliberate gap at the center. The gap is the brand's only recurring graphic motif. It must never become a three-pointed star or wheel badge. The wordmark is set large, tightly tracked, and cropped by the viewport in campaign use. Shopping UI uses a conventional readable sans serif.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Night graphite | `#111418` | Core dark ground |
| Carbon | `#1D2226` | Raised surfaces |
| Mercury | `#BFC7CC` | Rules, hardware cues |
| Bone | `#F1EEE7` | Primary text and light mode ground |
| Signal citron | `#D9F24A` | Main action and one campaign moment |
| Soft graphite | `#697278` | Supporting copy |

**Typography:** A heavy condensed system sans for campaign words, with tight tracking; a clean sans for navigation, product data, and body text. Use a large scale jump between campaign type and commerce copy.

**Spacing:** 8 px base rhythm; 24 px card padding; 64–112 px between major desktop sections. Product and navigation controls stay at least 44 px high.

**Radii:** 2 px on campaign image frames, 10 px on cards and dialogs, pill shape only for small controls. Do not apply one radius to every surface.

**Motion:** 120 ms for hover feedback, 200 ms for theme and selection changes, 280 ms for quick-view dialog. Animate opacity and transform; respect reduced motion.

## Campaign and product images

The hero uses a right-weighted model composition so type sits in dark negative space. Product imagery uses consistent cool gray studio backgrounds and physically plausible garments. The citron portrait is reserved for the lookbook: one high-energy break in an otherwise controlled sequence. Keep text as HTML, not baked into images.

## Components

- **Header:** compact wordmark, three anchor links, theme switch, demo bag.
- **Hero:** large cropped wordmark, one line of brand proposition, one primary collection action.
- **Product card:** full garment image, one descriptor, name, indicative concept price, quick view.
- **Quick view:** size choice, material and fit information, demo bag action.
- **Concept index:** lists the other four worlds as future chapters without linking to unfinished sites.

## Commerce status

Prices, inventory, and bag behavior are illustrative for portfolio demonstration. The site has no payment or fulfillment integration. Before a commercial launch, review naming and visual marks, establish real products and policies, and connect a commerce backend.

## Asset manifest

All five images in `public/images/` were generated for this concept. The product images are concept renders rather than photographs of manufactured garments.
