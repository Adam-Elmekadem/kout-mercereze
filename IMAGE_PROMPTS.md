# Mercereze image generation briefs

All five assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool and saved into `public/images/` as optimized WebP files. They are concept visuals, not evidence of manufactured garments.

## `hero.webp`

Wide editorial photograph: composed adult model on the right wearing a long engineered graphite-black coat with bonded wool, nylon faille insets, sculpted shoulder, high collar, reflective piping, and brushed-silver closure. Dark architectural studio, cool edge light, detailed shadows, left-side negative space for website copy. Original angular split-M embroidery only; no existing automotive marks, cars, text, or watermark.

## `grand-tourer-coat.webp`

Portrait 4:5 catalog photograph: full-length graphite sculptural overcoat on an invisible mannequin, seamless warm gray backdrop. Believable bonded-wool weight, asymmetrical collar, technical insets, silver seams and waist closure, small original split-M. Large softbox and edge light, complete garment visible, no text or existing brand symbols.

## `velocity-bomber.webp`

Portrait 4:5 catalog photograph: cropped charcoal calf-leather bomber with natural grain, black technical nylon sleeves, ribbed trim, precise panel seams, silver zip, and original small split-M embroidery. Invisible mannequin, pale silver-gray studio background, controlled softbox and edge light, physically plausible construction.

## `track-knit.webp`

Portrait 4:5 catalog photograph: heavyweight charcoal loopback-cotton crewneck with structured shoulder, reflective piping, original angular split-M chest mark, and a small citron woven tab. Invisible mannequin, cool stone-gray background, visible knit texture and natural folds, directional studio light.

## `editorial.webp`

Vertical fashion portrait: adult model in the charcoal technical bomber, direct calm gaze, natural skin detail, cropped head to upper torso. Flat electric citron studio background, soft frontal flash and silver rim, garment leather grain and hardware visible. No typography, existing logos, cars, or watermark.
